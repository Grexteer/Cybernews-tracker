About CyberNews Tracker

Cybersecurity changes every day. CyberNews Tracker collects current news on vulnerabilities, ransomware, data breaches, malware, phishing and security research in one place, so you do not have to visit many sites.

Where the news comes from

The articles are real, current news from many publishers, collected through the NewsAPI. We show the headline, a short excerpt and the source. The full story always stays with the publisher: every article links to its original source.

Topics are assigned automatically from keywords in the headline, so a category can occasionally be off.

What you can do

  • Search the news and filter by topic or source.
  • Create a free account and save articles to read later – they wait for you in your dashboard.
  • Administrators can import fresh news and add, edit or remove articles.

Built with

Python and Flask, a SQLite database, and plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Passwords are stored as hashes, forms are protected against CSRF, and repeated failed logins are blocked.
